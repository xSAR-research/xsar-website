About xSAR

xSAR is a small research effort based in Toowoomba. We look at ideas that could support emergency service and SES teams across QLD and NT without adding to their load. Everything stays in the background. Our only goal is to honour the skill and commitment of the people who turn up when communities need them most.

The heroes we support

The real strength of the emergency service and the SES lies with ordinary people who train hard and leave home at a moment’s notice. They bring local knowledge, steady hands and genuine care. We hold their service in high regard and keep their daily realities at the centre of anything we consider.

How we try to help

We explore tools that might help spot someone sooner or keep teams connected in difficult conditions. Any progress we make belongs to the volunteers who use it. We stay out of the way and focus only on what might genuinely lighten the load for those on the ground.

Our approach

Safety first for rescuers

Nothing we do should ever increase risk to the people on the ground.

Reliability in the field

Ideas only matter if they work when conditions are tough and resources are limited.

Humble service

We are here to support, not lead. The volunteers and the communities they serve come first.

Thank you to every volunteer who gives their time to the State Emergency Services in Queensland and the Northern Territory. Your courage and generosity keep communities safe. xSAR is simply here to offer quiet, steady support for that work.
