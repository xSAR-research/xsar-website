Practical autonomous systems for search and rescue.

xSAR is an independent philanthropic engineering project based in Toowoomba. We research drones, sensors, positioning, communications and open-source tools that may help emergency responders work with better information and less avoidable risk. Human oversight remains central.

Current focus · July 2026

CAN FD node communications, RP2350 firmware, sensor acquisition, GNSS evaluation and Raspberry Pi 5 integration. Work is dated and published as it becomes useful, reproducible and documented.
