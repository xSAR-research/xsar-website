Saving lives one drone at a time.

xSAR researches AI-assisted autonomous drone swarms to extend situational awareness for rescuers in Queensland and the Northern Territory. When bushfires rage, floods rise, storms strike or people go missing, emergency services and the State Emergency Service (SES) answer the call with local knowledge and steady hands. We work in the background to deliver practical swarm support that might stand beside them on the ground—always with human-in-the-loop oversight.

Information for decision makers Contact the team

How we try to help

Autonomous drone swarms give ground teams an extra set of eyes over wide or difficult country. Multispectral and thermal imaging can detect a heat signature when visibility is poor, so searchers spend more time where it matters and less time exposed.

Eyes in the sky

Coordinated drone swarms scan large areas quickly and stream live situational awareness back to mobile command.

Safer for everyone

Operate in conditions where manned aircraft or foot teams would be delayed or at greater risk.

Quiet support

We're at the forefront of AI-assisted swarm technology, with human-in-the-loop safety, oversight and control.
