Open-source engineering

xSAR publishes practical software, firmware and research tools when they are useful enough to inspect, build, test or extend. Some early hardware experiments, unstable firmware and raw datasets remain offline until they are documented well enough for a responsible release.

The public repository map is organised by engineering area and uses plain status labels so visitors can tell the difference between an idea, an experiment and something ready to use.

Engineering areas

Cross-platform tools

Portable Rust utilities for Linux, macOS and Windows.

Firmware

Rust firmware for RP2350 sensing, control and communications nodes.

Linux tools

Utilities for storage, services, automation and systems integration.

Sensors

Acquisition, analogue measurement, battery monitoring and sensor-node work.

Positioning and navigation

GNSS evaluation, timing and integration with mobile and flight systems.

Communications and DSP

CAN FD, mesh, RF and signal-processing work for connected field systems.
