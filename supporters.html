Supporters

xSAR is a one-person philanthropic research effort, but worthwhile work rarely grows alone. Support may be infrastructure, equipment, specialist knowledge, testing, introductions, funding or a short message that says the work matters.

Every contribution is treated as part of the project story, not as a logo added to the end of a page.

Migadu professional email hosting

Migadu provides the private, no-tracking email hosting that keeps xSAR communication professional and under our own domain. Their Swiss-based, independently funded service is built on open standards, respects privacy, and never treats mailboxes as advertising inventory. That is exactly the kind of steady, ethical infrastructure a public-benefit project needs.
Pay it forward and support Migadu at Migadu.com.

Ways to help

Practical support

Infrastructure, hosting, equipment, services or access to useful test facilities.

Engineering support

Technical review, specialist advice, testing, field knowledge or useful introductions.

Human support

Funding helps, but so does encouragement. A short message or thoughtful conversation is genuinely welcome.

Join the story

Something on the site or in the open-source work may connect with your experience. You are welcome to start a conversation, with no pressure and no expectation.

Supporters, major milestones and useful collaborations are also acknowledged through X @xSAR_Research.

Pay it forward. We may all need help one day.
