Supporters

xSAR is a small philanthropic research effort. The organisations and people below help us keep the lights on and the work focused on quiet, practical support for emergency services and the State Emergency Service (SES) in Queensland and the Northern Territory. We are grateful for every bit of solid infrastructure and goodwill that stands behind that mission.

Migadu professional email hosting

Migadu provides the private, no-tracking email hosting that keeps xSAR communication professional and under our own domain. Their Swiss-based, bootstrapped service is built on open standards, respects privacy, and never treats mailboxes as advertising inventory — exactly the kind of steady, ethical infrastructure a public-benefit project needs when coordinating with rescuers and partners.
Pay it forward and support Migadu at Migadu.com

xSAR operates as a one-person philanthropic effort to leverage technology to help those when they need it the most. If you can offer infrastructure, tools, funding, or practical advice that helps this work stay independent and useful to rescuers, I would be glad to hear from you via the Contact page — no pressure, and no expectation. A short encouraging message is often enough or if something on this site or my public open-source repository resonates.

Pay it forward - we all might need help one day.
