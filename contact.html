Contact xSAR

You do not need a formal proposal or a large contribution to get in touch. Messages are welcome from emergency-service personnel, engineers, researchers, potential supporters, people with useful equipment or services, and anyone who simply wants to say hello.

Engineering

Technical discussion, review, testing, field experience or collaboration.

Support

Infrastructure, equipment, services, introductions, funding or practical advice.

Conversation

Questions, encouragement or a connection to someone who may find the work useful.

Background and approach

  • Experience: More than 45 years building and running systems, from early machine code and embedded work to enterprise platforms.
  • RF and electronics: Advanced Amateur Radio Licence holder with practical RF, electronics and hardware experience.
  • Systems thinking: Comfortable moving between circuit boards, Linux systems, software architecture and field constraints.
  • Engineering style: Proven, maintainable designs over fashion-driven stacks, with clear testing and honest documentation.
