Contact xSAR

If you serve with emergency services or the State Emergency Service (SES) in Queensland or Northern Territory, or you support those who do, we would value hearing from you. Your experience on the ground matters more than any idea we might have. Please share your thoughts or contact us if you want more information on this life-saving project.

Background & expertise

  • Experience: More than 45 years building and running systems—from early machine code on the VIC-20 through bare-metal and embedded work to enterprise-grade platforms. The thread is the same: careful engineering that holds up when the environment is unforgiving.
  • Certifications: MCSA and MCSD (Microsoft Certified Systems Architect and Solutions Developer), grounding long practice in formal systems and solution design.
  • RF / electronics: Advanced Amateur Radio Licence holder for over 45 years. Hands-on experience with RF propagation, electronics, and hardware-level electrical engineering—skills that sit naturally beside field radios, sensors, and low-power devices.
  • Approach: Comfortable moving between circuit boards and large deployments. Prefers proven, maintainable designs over fashion-driven stacks, and keeps the focus on tools that quietly help people who work under pressure.

After sending we will communicate with you via email first. Thank you.
