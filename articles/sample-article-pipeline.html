Why search theory needs maths

A classic result in search theory is Koopman’s random search formula. With sweep width WW, track length LL and search area AA, the coverage is c=WLAc = \frac{WL}{A} and the probability of detection is:

POD=1−e−WL/A \mathrm{POD} = 1 - e^{-WL/A}

Summing over segments ii, the overall probability of success is

POS=∑i=1nPOAi×PODi \mathrm{POS} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathrm{POA}_i \times \mathrm{POD}_i
